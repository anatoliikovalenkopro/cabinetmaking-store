FAQ

Good questions

How much do custom cabinets cost?

Every project is different, but we give a fixed, itemized quote after your free consultation. Cost depends on size, materials, door style and finish. We're happy to design around a budget.

How long does a project take?

Most kitchens take 6–10 weeks from approved design to installation. Vanities and small built-ins are often 3–5 weeks; custom tables 4–8 weeks depending on the slab and finish.

Do you handle permits or work with my contractor?

Cabinetry itself doesn't usually need a permit, but we coordinate with your general contractor, plumber and electrician, and work to your renovation or basement-development schedule.

Do you install countertops?

Yes. We supply and install quartz, granite, solid surface and butcher-block tops through trusted Alberta fabricators, so you deal with one team.

Do you serve areas outside Calgary and Edmonton?

Yes — we work across Alberta, including the Bow Valley, central Alberta, southern Alberta and rural acreages. Travel may apply for distant locations.

What warranty do you offer?

All our work carries a 5-year workmanship warranty, and hardware is covered by the manufacturer's lifetime warranty where applicable.

Do you offer financing?

We take a deposit to start and the balance on completion. Ask us about financing options for larger projects.
